EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is entered into on 01-07-2026 by and between:
1. ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd., having its registered office at Tech Park, Bangalore (hereinafter referred to as the "Company"), and
2. John Smith, residing at 42 Maple Street (hereinafter referred to as the "Employee").

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ the Employee, and the Employee desires to accept employment under the following terms and conditions:

1. PURPOSE & POSITION:
To outline the terms and conditions of employment for John Smith as a Senior Software Engineer.

2. DURATION & TERM:
The term of this agreement shall be for 12 months starting from 01-07-2026, subject to renewal upon mutual consent.

3. COMPENSATION & PAYMENT TERMS:
The Company shall pay the Employee a monthly salary of ₹50,000, payable on the last business day of each calendar month via direct deposit.

4. EMPLOYEE OBLIGATIONS:
The Employee agrees to perform assigned duties and follow company policies diligently. The Employee shall maintain confidentiality regarding company information and proprietary software source code.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & RIGHTS:
Any intellectual property created during employment belongs to the company. The Employee assigns all rights, title, and interest in work product to ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

6. TERMINATION CONDITIONS:
Either party may terminate by providing 30 days written notice, or payment of salary in lieu thereof. In case of gross misconduct, the Company reserves the right of immediate termination.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY:
The Employee shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all company trade secrets, customer records, and proprietary technologies during and after the period of employment.

8. GOVERNING LAW:
This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.
